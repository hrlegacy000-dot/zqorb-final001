01 / Game / Active development

DETENTION

THE NEGATIVITY CURSE — a psychological horror action adventure about a living school formed from a century of fear, pressure, regret, failure and self-doubt.

Single player 4-player co-op Unreal Engine 5 Psychological horror
The school is the feeling.

Astraeon High is not just a location. It is a living environment shaped by academic pressure and negative emotion. Every threat should map back to something recognisable: judgment, comparison, failure, regret or the fear of being forgotten.
