02 / OVERVIEW

A CURSE BUILT
FROM WHAT WE
DON'T SAY.

DETENTION is a psychological action-horror game about a school where a century of academic pressure has curdled into something that hunts. Every threat in the game maps back to a real, nameable feeling.

Story-first. Character-first. No filler scares.

03 / WORLD
01

ASTRAEON HIGH

A school carrying a century of academic trauma, arriving at a fixed point the game calls Result Day.

02

THE NULL

The presence behind the curse. Its visual language is reserved and deliberate — never overused.

03

THE FOURTH DIMENSION

Time inside the curse doesn't behave normally. It's felt, not measured — never given a hard number.
